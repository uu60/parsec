# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect
privacy, and accept maintainer decisions made to protect the community. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the non-public contact method listed on their GitHub
profiles. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, while recognizing that investigation may require limited
disclosure.
